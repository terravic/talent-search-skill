# Sample Prompts for Talent Search & Executive Intelligence

Use these sample prompts directly in Gemini Enterprise App, Antigravity, or any AI assistant equipped with the `talent-search` skill.

---

## Prompt 1: Healthcare CIO with AI & Cloud Focus
```text
I need to profile 5 leading Chief Information Officers (CIOs) or Chief Digital Officers (CDOs) at major US health systems who are actively pioneering generative AI and cloud modernizations. Please output only the standardized Markdown table with verified public sources.
```

---

## Prompt 2: Chief Actuary in Health Insurance (Value-Based Care)
```text
Source and profile 5 Chief Actuaries or Heads of Actuarial Services at major US health plans (e.g., Elevance Health, Centene, Cigna, Humana, Oscar Health) with deep expertise in Medicare Advantage risk adjustment, value-based care pricing, and predictive claims analytics. Output strictly as the executive Markdown table.
```

---

## Prompt 3: VP of Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare / Digital Health
```text
Identify 5 VP-level leaders in Healthcare AI and Clinical Machine Learning currently working in digital health, biopharma, or top health systems. Focus on leaders who have successfully deployed clinical LLMs or FDA-cleared AI algorithms. Present the results in the standardized 7-column table.
```

---

## Prompt 4: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) in Hospital Systems
```text
Find 5 Chief Information Security Officers (CISOs) leading cybersecurity at large multi-hospital health systems in the US. Highlight their experience in medical device security, zero trust architecture, and ransomware resilience. Provide only the executive Markdown table.
```

---

## Prompt 5: Chief Medical Officer (CMO) driving Digital Clinical Transformation
```text
Profile 5 Chief Medical Officers (CMOs) or Chief Medical Information Officers (CMIOs) who have led major clinical workflow transformations, ambient voice AI documentation rollouts, or physician burnout reduction initiatives at major health networks. Format strictly as a single Markdown table.
```

---

## Prompt 6: Large-Scale Talent Mapping Strategy (Batch Approach)
When mapping a large talent pool (e.g., 20+ executives), execute the search in batches of 5 to preserve search depth and avoid rate limits:

**Batch 1 (Academic Medical Centers):**
```text
Profile 5 CIOs from top Academic Medical Centers (e.g., Mayo Clinic, Johns Hopkins, Yale New Haven Health, Stanford Health Care, Cleveland Clinic). Output table only.
```

**Batch 2 (Integrated Delivery Networks):**
```text
Profile 5 CIOs from large regional Integrated Delivery Networks (e.g., Kaiser Permanente, Intermountain Health, Providence, CommonSpirit, Novant Health). Output table only.
```

**Batch 3 (Pediatric & Specialized Systems):**
```text
Profile 5 CIOs from leading specialized or pediatric health systems (e.g., Seattle Children's, Boston Children's, MD Anderson, Memorial Sloan Kettering). Output table only.
```
